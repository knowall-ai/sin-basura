== Executive Summary

Sin Basura ("Without Trash") is a community-driven litter collection program for El Salvador that rewards participants with Bitcoin via Lightning payments. The program uses the Nostr protocol for transparent operations and Geyser for trustless crowdfunding.

=== Vision

A cleaner El Salvador powered by Bitcoin incentives and decentralized verification.

=== Mission

To incentivize and reward community members for collecting litter, creating both environmental impact and economic opportunity through Bitcoin micropayments.

== Problem Statement

=== Environmental Challenge

El Salvador faces significant litter and waste management challenges, particularly in tourist areas, beaches, and urban communities. Traditional cleanup programs rely on:

* Volunteer goodwill (unsustainable)
* Government funding (limited and bureaucratic)
* NGO programs (dependent on grants)

=== Economic Opportunity

Many Salvadorans, particularly in rural and coastal areas, have:

* Limited formal employment opportunities
* Access to Bitcoin/Lightning via the Chivo wallet and other apps
* Time and willingness to participate in community improvement

=== Trust Problem

Previous incentive programs have struggled with:

* Fraud and fake submissions
* Centralized fund management (corruption risk)
* Lack of transparency in fund distribution
* Delayed or missing payments

== Solution

Sin Basura addresses these challenges through:

[cols="1,2"]
|===
| Challenge | Solution

| Sustainability
| Bitcoin incentives create ongoing motivation

| Verification
| Local attesters verify collections in person

| Trust
| All transactions visible on Nostr; funds protected by smart contracts

| Payments
| Instant Lightning zaps upon verification

| Transparency
| Every collection, attestation, and payment is a public Nostr event
|===

== Target Users

=== Collectors

**Who:** Community members who collect litter

**Demographics:**

* Age: 16-65
* Location: El Salvador (initially coastal and urban areas)
* Tech: Smartphone with Nostr client and Lightning wallet

**Needs:**

* Earn income for productive work
* Simple submission process
* Fast, reliable payments
* Recognition for contributions

**User Journey:**

1. Download Nostr client (Damus, Amethyst, Primal)
2. Set up Lightning address (Wallet of Satoshi, Blink, Alby)
3. Find litter in community
4. Collect into bags
5. Take photo with location
6. Post to Nostr with `#sinbasura` tag
7. Wait for attester verification
8. Receive sats instantly

=== Attesters

**Who:** Trusted local verifiers who confirm collections

**Demographics:**

* Age: 25-55
* Location: Specific regions of El Salvador
* Reputation: Known community members, business owners, or local leaders

**Needs:**

* Clear verification criteria
* Simple attestation process
* Recognition for service
* Possible compensation (future)

**Responsibilities:**

* Verify collections in person when possible
* Check photo evidence for authenticity
* Reject fraudulent submissions
* Report suspicious patterns

=== Donors

**Who:** Individuals and organizations funding the program

**Demographics:**

* Bitcoiners worldwide
* Environmental organizations
* El Salvador tourism businesses
* Corporate social responsibility programs

**Needs:**

* Assurance funds are used properly
* Transparency on impact
* Tax documentation (future)
* Recognition for contributions

**Contribution Methods:**

* Geyser All-or-Nothing campaign (smart contract protection)
* Direct Nostr zaps to the Zap Goal
* Corporate sponsorship (future)

== Risks & Mitigations

[cols="1,2,2,1"]
|===
| Risk | Description | Mitigation | Severity

| Fraud
| Fake submissions, recycled photos
| In-person attestation, photo metadata verification, rate limiting
| High

| Attester Collusion
| Attesters approving fake collections
| Multiple attesters per region, reputation tracking, audits
| Medium

| Fund Depletion
| More collections than funds available
| Real-time balance visibility, pause when low, continuous fundraising
| Medium

| Low Adoption
| Collectors don't participate
| Marketing, community outreach, competitive rates
| Medium

| Technical Failure
| Relay downtime, payment failures
| Multiple relays, retry logic, manual fallback
| Low
|===

== Glossary

[cols="1,2"]
|===
| Term | Definition

| Attester
| Authorized local verifier who confirms litter collections in person

| Collector
| Community member who picks up litter and submits proof

| Nostr
| Decentralized protocol for social networking and messaging

| Zap
| Bitcoin Lightning payment sent via Nostr

| Zap Goal
| Crowdfunding target on Nostr (NIP-75)

| NIP
| Nostr Implementation Possibility (protocol specification)

| Pubkey
| Nostr public key identifying a user

| Relay
| Server that stores and distributes Nostr events

| Sats
| Satoshis, the smallest unit of Bitcoin (1 BTC = 100,000,000 sats)
|===
